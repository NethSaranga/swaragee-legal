SwaraGee — Privacy Policy

This document describes what the app and its backend actually do, written against the code as it stands on the date above. It is not legal advice. If SwaraGee takes on users at scale in the EEA or UK, have a solicitor read it.

1 · Who we are

SwaraGee is a guitar chords and tabs app for Sinhala songs, published by Saranga Jayaneth, based in Sri Lanka.

Your favourites, setlists, your own original songs, and any chord sheets you submit. These are yours. Originals and setlists are stored on our servers as well as on your phone so that losing a phone does not lose your songwriting.

3.4 The microphone — the tuner, metronome and ear-training tools

The tuner needs the microphone to hear your guitar. It does not record.

Audio is analysed entirely on your phone by code built into the app. What leaves that process is a single number — the pitch it detected, several times a second — which is used to move the needle on screen and is then gone. No audio is written to storage, and none is sent to us or to anyone else. There is nothing to upload because nothing is kept.

The permission is asked for only when you first open a tool that needs it, and the app works without it apart from those tools.

3.5 Advertising — free accounts only

Free accounts see ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google may use an advertising identifier and similar device data to select them. What Google collects, and what it does with it, is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and its partner-site policy.

  • In the EEA and the UK, you are shown a consent form before any personalised ad loads. Declining means contextual ads instead — not a broken app, and not a worse app except in the obvious way.
  • On iOS, Apple separately asks whether SwaraGee may track you across other apps. You can decline, and you can change it later in Settings.
  • In certain US states, you are shown an equivalent choice.
  • Pro subscribers see no ads at all. The ad code is not merely hidden — for a Pro, trial or promotional account the advertising SDK is never started, no advertising identifier is read, and no request leaves the device.

3.6 Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google through their own stores, and by RevenueCat, which tells us whether your subscription is active. We never see your card details. We receive the fact of a purchase, its product and its expiry.

4 · What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data, and we have no arrangement with any data broker.
  • We do not use your data to train machine-learning models.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, beyond the advertising described in §3.5 — which you can decline.
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children. SwaraGee is a general-audience music app, is not directed at children, and is not marked as child-directed in any ad network. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to the address in §1 and we will delete it.

7 · Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See what we hold about you
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete your account and everything personal in it
  • Export your data in a portable form
  • Object to or restrict how we use it

Account deletion is built into the app — you do not have to email anyone. Settings → your profile → delete your account. Your data is marked for deletion immediately, stops being visible at once, and is permanently removed after a 30-day grace period, which exists so an accidental or compromised deletion can be undone.

For anything else, write to the address in §1. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our lawful bases are: contract for running your account, legitimate interests for keeping the service working and secure, and consent for personalised advertising and push notifications — both of which you can withdraw at any time, in the app or in your phone's settings. You also have the right to complain to your national data-protection authority.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy statute, you have equivalent rights of access, deletion and correction, and the right to opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising — which is the choice offered in §3.5. We do not sell personal information.

8 · Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain and the Android Keystore, never in ordinary app storage. Access to the production database is restricted and administrative actions are logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.
